Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

1. Introduction

PreparaThor is an independent educational application created by Ihar Karunny. It is designed to help users prepare for technical interviews and assess their knowledge.

We respect your privacy. The core functionality of PreparaThor works locally on your device. We do not operate a user account system or our own server for storing your questions, answers, test results, or other personal content.

2. Information We Process

PreparaThor itself does not ask you to provide personal information such as your name, address, or account credentials.

However, the application integrates third-party services for analytics and advertising. These services may process technical and usage information needed to provide analytics, measure advertising performance, display advertisements, prevent abuse, and maintain service reliability.

  • Yandex AppMetrica may process information such as app and device information, operating system information, app version, usage and interaction data, diagnostics, and device identifiers depending on the SDK configuration.
  • Yandex Mobile Ads may process information required to deliver and measure advertisements. Access to Apple's IDFA is subject to Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission requirements.

We do not intentionally collect precise location, contacts, photos, microphone data, or other sensitive personal information through PreparaThor.

3. Information Stored Locally

PreparaThor keeps its question bank and the information required for its core functionality on your device. Test generation and the processing of your answers and results are performed locally using the content included with the application.

We do not maintain a server-side database containing your test history or answers.

4. Analytics and Advertising

We use Yandex AppMetrica to understand how the application is used, diagnose technical issues, and improve the product. We use Yandex Mobile Ads to display advertisements and support the free version of the application.

Third-party SDKs may collect information independently of us according to their own technical systems and privacy practices. We configure these services in accordance with the permissions and privacy mechanisms provided by Apple and the applicable requirements.

5. Tracking and Advertising Identifiers

PreparaThor does not access Apple's Advertising Identifier (IDFA) unless the required permission is granted through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. If permission is not granted, the application does not use IDFA for tracking.

Other technical identifiers, such as Apple's Identifier for Vendor (IDFV), may be available to integrated analytics services as part of their standard operation.

6. AI-Assisted Content

AI tools may have been used during the preparation, refinement, or expansion of some questions and educational materials. AI is not used interactively by the application. Your answers, test results, or other user data are not sent from PreparaThor to AI services.

AI-assisted questions are reviewed by people and are identified accordingly within the application.

7. Purchases and Subscriptions

Premium purchases and subscriptions are processed through Apple's StoreKit framework and Apple's App Store infrastructure. PreparaThor does not receive or store your payment card, banking, or other payment credentials.

8. Support by Email

If you choose to contact us using the in-app support feature, PreparaThor opens Apple's system mail composer. The email is sent using your own email account. PreparaThor does not operate a support server and does not store the contents of support emails.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Because PreparaThor does not maintain its own account or server-side database for user content, we do not have a central user profile or personal-content database to delete. Information stored locally can generally be removed by deleting the application from your device, subject to Apple's system behavior and any information retained by third-party services under their own policies.

For information processed by third-party analytics or advertising providers, their respective privacy policies and retention practices apply.

10. Children's Privacy

PreparaThor is a general-purpose educational application focused on technical interview preparation. It is not specifically directed to children and does not intentionally request personal information from children.

11. Third-Party Services

PreparaThor uses services provided by third parties, including Apple and Yandex. Their handling of information is governed by their respective terms and privacy policies.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application, its features, or its data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.
